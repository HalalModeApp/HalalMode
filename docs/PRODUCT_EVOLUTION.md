# Product evolution: meaningful momentum

Halal Mode is not an infinite-feed dating product. Retention is earned through
clarity, progress, and respectful anticipation, never scarcity pressure,
social comparison, or compulsive checking.

## The member loop

1. A deliberate daily set arrives with the Madinah-Fajr cycle.
2. A member can read, keep, release, or express interest privately.
3. Mutual interest opens the five-question, double-blind icebreaker.
4. A broad compatibility overview surfaces values, marriage timing, location
   and relocation, family plans, and conversation as shared areas to explore.
5. Members can continue, pause, close, block, or report with equal clarity.

## Ethical engagement rules

- Use visual delight, thoughtful prompts, and private progress feedback; never
  use streak-loss anxiety, hidden quotas, misleading availability, or ranking.
- Make every notification consent-based, understandable, quiet-hour aware, and
  easy to turn off. It should invite a meaningful action, not pull someone
  into an endless feed.
- Celebrate completed choices and mutual milestones privately. Do not show
  popularity counts, attractiveness scores, leaderboards, or comparisons.
- Explain limits plainly. Daily-set and connection limits protect focus and
  safety; they must not disguise a purchase wall.

## Premium value without coercion

Halal Mode Premium can improve clarity and control: a fuller daily set,
additional releases, advanced reciprocal filters, more open conversations, and
profile-management tools. It must never sell access to a person who did not
reciprocate, visibility outside preferences, safety protections, or a way
around consent.

## Future experiments, after measurement and review

| Concept | Member benefit | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gentle daily reflection | Helps members explain what mattered in a set. | Optional, private, skippable, and never framed as a score. |
| Stage-aware conversation prompts | Helps a connection move from icebreaker to practical discussion. | Both members see the same prompt; no sensitive inference. |
| Compatibility discussion checklist | Turns “worth discussing” into useful next questions. | Reveals no private filter, score, or hidden rationale. |
| Thoughtful return reminder | Helps a member return to a connection they opened. | One clear reminder, quiet hours, and permanent opt-out. |

## Explicit non-goals

- No family or wali circle.
- No readiness program.
- No paid relocation opt-in: cross-country introductions are reciprocal when
  both members are open to each other's countries.
- No opaque member-facing score, shadow ranking, or explanation of why a
  particular person was matched.
